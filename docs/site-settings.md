# Site name, favicon and sharing

These all live under **Settings** (the gear button).

## Site name

The site name is what appears in the browser tab and bookmarks, in search
results, in link previews on WhatsApp, iMessage, Slack and the like, and
under the icon when someone adds the page to their phone's home screen. The
settings show a little preview of the tab and the link preview as you type.
Leave it empty to use the name from your profile.

## Description

Shown in search results and link previews. If you leave it empty, the start
of your bio is used.

## Favicon

The default is the Trellis icon. You can pick an emoji instead (it's turned
into a crisp SVG icon) or upload a square image; a 512 × 512 PNG is ideal. An
uploaded image is also used as the home-screen icon.

## Link previews

When someone shares your page, chat apps and social networks show a card
with the site name, description and your profile photo. **Settings > Link
preview image** lets you choose a different picture, cropped to the
1200 × 630 those apps expect.

If the page is hidden from crawlers (see
[Search engines and AI](privacy-and-crawlers.md)), link previews are blocked
as well, unless you tick **Still allow link previews in chat apps**.

## The "Powered by Trellis" footer

There's a small "Powered by Trellis" link at the bottom of the page. Untick
**Settings > Footer > Show "Powered by Trellis"** to remove it. It's set per
page, so you can keep it on your own and turn it off on a client's.
